- Latency tool (item 3): `ftrd-stream --latency-test N`. Everything on the Frame's clock, no
  camera: park the PC cursor at (3/4, 3/4) of the virtual monitor for 300-333 ms (random, so
  moves land at random points of the capture cycle), send an absolute move to (1/4, 1/4), and
  time until the first decoded frame whose 24x32 luma box at that spot differs from the frames
  before (>= 20 pixels changed by > 40 levels). Path covered: Frame input send -> network -> PC
  cursor -> capture -> NVENC -> network -> iris decode. FTRD_LAT_CONTROL=1 sends the cursor
  elsewhere as a negative control.

Measured (Remote Monitor 2560x1440 HEVC 50 Mb/s, 90 fps asked, `--novr`, 2 runs x 30 trials per
path, alternated dongle/home/dongle/home, 2026-10-08 ~12:20):

| path                              | trials | median  | p10   | p90   | min   | max    | misses |
|-----------------------------------|--------|---------|-------|-------|-------|--------|--------|
| Valve USB adapter (10.35.78.22)   | 58/60  | 13.1 ms | 11.2  | 25.4  | 10.7  | 49.9   | 2      |
| home Wi-Fi (10.0.0.65)            | 55/60  | 19.2 ms | 15.4  | 38.0  | 12.2  | 344.1  | 5      |

- Host capture+encode median 2.3-2.5 ms, decode 2.6-3.0 ms, both paths: the ~6 ms gap is the
  network (and its jitter: home Wi-Fi had 217 and 344 ms outliers, the adapter none above 50).
- Negative control (cursor sent elsewhere, 5 trials): 4 misses, 1 false hit at 687 ms (a later
  frame changed the box: something else on the PC's desktop, or a delayed cursor redraw). So a
  hit within ~50 ms is the cursor; misses in the real runs (2 and 5 of 60) are moves whose
  redraw didn't show within 1 s or that Sunshine coalesced, excluded from the numbers.
- Earlier 10-trial check at 1920x1080 on the adapter: median 14.0 ms.
- PC layout after all runs: ultrawide primary at 0,0, 1080p at 1622,1440, no virtual monitor
  left over.

What the number is and isn't:
- It is input-to-decoded-frame on the Frame. Not included: the Frame-side time from a laser /
  mouse event to the send (sub-ms in ftrd-stream), handing the frame to KWin/SteamVR (window
  mode: receive->shown 3.8 ms already counts most of that; overlay: +1.7 ms convert), and the
  SteamVR compositor + display scan-out (~1 frame at 90 Hz, ~11 ms, plus up to another frame of
  waiting), which only a camera can see.
- Estimated mouse-to-photon: adapter ~13 + 1-2 + 11-22 = **~25-37 ms**; home Wi-Fi
  ~19 + 1-2 + 11-22 = **~31-43 ms**. Both under the 50-70 ms comfort bar. A camera photo would
  pin the display part; not done.
